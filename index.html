About Soulbent

Soulbent Project is a registered community-driven environmental NPO based in Saulsville, Atteridgeville, Pretoria. Founded by siblings Mashudu Makhado, Millicent Makhado, and the late Tshifhiwa Makhado, it began as a small family effort and has grown into a movement of hundreds of residents.

“As residents, we realised that waiting for change was not enough, we had to become the change.” — Mashudu Makhado, CEO & Co-founder

Soulbent tackles illegal dumping, river pollution, and food insecurity through hands-on clean-ups, recycling, and food gardens that turn reclaimed dumpsites into sources of fresh produce for the community.

Cemetery Restoration

Saulsville Cemetery and other resting places cleaned and restored with dignity.

Food Gardens

Spinach, carrots, onions, tomatoes — grown on reclaimed land for food security.
