What We Do

Six core programmes that turn environmental action into community transformation.

Illegal Dumping Clean-ups

Regular clean-ups across Saulsville, Atteridgeville, Soweto, and beyond.

Volunteers removing waste from a dumpsite

River & Wetland Clean-ups

Monangwe river and other waterways restored with 80+ volunteers per event.

Team in waders clearing river pollution

Cemetery Restoration

Saulsville Cemetery and other resting places cleaned and restored with dignity.

Volunteers tidying a cemetery section

Food Gardens

Spinach, carrots, onions, tomatoes — grown on reclaimed land for food security.

Lush vegetable beds with volunteers harvesting

Soup Kitchen & Food Relief

Meals for school children, food vouchers, and parcels for ~1,000 families.

Community members receiving meals

Recycling & Awareness

PET bottle collection, anti-littering campaigns, and anti-bullying education.
